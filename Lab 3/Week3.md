### Standards Lecture (9/9)
After Wednesday's lecture, I realized how much we take standards for granted. It was a reminder, however, of how far we've come. 

I have always known that standards are thought-out and represent a collection of lessons learned over the course of our existence. Ignoring them means wasting resources repeating the same mistakes made decades or centuries before just to learn those same lessons. Standards today are thought-out and ignoring them simly means you're ignoring all the lessons learned. You are most likely wasting time, money, and resources repeating the same mistakes just to learn the same lessons.

Fortunately for us, generations before us have made those mistakes and in many aspects, we are beyond guess and check. However, as we continue to innovate and step into unknown spaces, we may very well be the generation making mistakes that future ones will make new standards from. It's like a wheel and I'm all for it.
